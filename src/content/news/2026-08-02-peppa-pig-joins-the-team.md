We are delighted to announce that Peppa Pig has joined Starflight Dynamics as our inaugural Chief Snort Officer (CSO).

Peppa brings a wealth of experience in low-altitude flight dynamics — specifically the parabolic trajectory achieved by jumping in muddy puddles — and we believe this translates directly to our atmospheric re-entry programme. Her older brother George has also expressed interest in joining the robotics division, pending a successful trial with a toy dinosaur integration test.

Peppa will be based at our Munich headquarters, where she has already made her mark by replacing the standard espresso machine with a more efficient mud-based energy system. Early results are promising.

When asked about her new role, Peppa said: *"Snort. Snort snort."*

We couldn't agree more, Peppa. Welcome to the team.
